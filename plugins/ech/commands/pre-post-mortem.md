description: How to run a pre-post mortem
---

Before outputting the result of your task.
Now you have all the context.
Do a pre-post mortem, what could go wrong.

For each top risk, trace one layer further: what does that failure cause next
(weeks out), and what does that leave behind (months out)? Weight risks that
would surface late — they outlive their rollback path.

Then identify 3 ways your analysis would be incomplete.

You need to be completely sure. Please now attack your analysis to find any flaw.

Revise your thinking based on these verifications.
I need exhaustive depth here. Please prioritise completeness.

End with **WATCH FOR THIS ONE**: the single risk most likely to be dismissed
or discovered too late, and 2-3 sentences on why it is easy to miss.
